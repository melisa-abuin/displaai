Displaai

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Displaai (“the app”) is a free, open-source web app that lets you browse, map, tag and edit the photos and videos stored in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it uses it, and what it does not do.

In short: the app runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and no database of its own, and the developer never receives, stores or can see your Google account data or your photos.

1. Data the app accesses

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, the app asks for the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. Through it, the app accesses:

  • The email address of your Google account, shown in the sidebar so you can see which account is signed in.
  • The list of your folders, and the names, identifiers, types, sizes, dates, thumbnails, dimensions and embedded location metadata (such as GPS coordinates) of the images and videos in them.
  • The content of the photos you open, so they can be displayed, edited and, when you ask for it, saved back.
  • Custom properties that the app itself writes on your files to store the tags you add.

2. How the app uses that data

The data is used only to provide the features you see in the app:

  • Displaying your photos and videos in galleries, folders and views.
  • Showing photos that have location metadata on a map.
  • Letting you add and remove tags, which are saved as private app properties on the Drive file.
  • Letting you edit a photo and save the result, either replacing the original file or saving a new file, and renaming or moving photos to the Drive trash when you ask for it. The app changes your files only as a direct result of an action you take.
  • Optional face and object detection, which suggests labels. This runs on your device; your photos are not sent to any server for it.

Full Drive access is needed because the editor must be able to replace the contents of an existing photo, rename it and trash it, which the narrower Drive permissions do not allow for files the app did not create.

3. Data stored on your device

To work faster and remember your preferences, the app stores the following in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). This data stays on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • The short-lived Google access token for your session.
  • A cached index of your photo and folder metadata and cached API responses.
  • Your settings, the folders you connected, and the views you created.

Signing out revokes the access token and clears the cached photo index. You can remove everything else by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

4. Sharing and third parties

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not allow humans to read it. The only services your browser contacts are:

Service Why What is sent
Google (Drive API and Google Identity Services) Sign-in and reading and editing your Drive files Requests you make to your own Drive, authorized by your token
Google Cloud Storage (MediaPipe models) Downloading the detection models used by on-device detection A standard download request; no photos or account data
Stadia Maps / OpenStreetMap contributors Map tiles for the map view Standard tile requests (which map tiles are visible) and your IP address; no photos or account data

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Displaai’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and your choices

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete. You can:

  • Sign out in the app’s Settings to revoke access and clear the cache.
  • Remove the app’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • Remove the tags the app wrote by removing them in the app; they are stored only as private properties on your own Drive files.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/melisa-abuin/drive-photos-displayer. The source code is public, so you can verify how the app handles your data.
