Displaai

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Displaai (“the app”, “we”) is a free, open-source web app published at displaai.mkdir.no by Melisa Abuin (“the developer”). It lets you browse, map, tag and edit the photos and videos stored in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, shared, stored and protected, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and no database of its own.
  • The developer never receives, stores or can see your Google account data or your photos.
  • The app accesses your Google Drive only after you sign in with Google and grant permission, and only to show, tag, edit and organize your own photos and videos.
  • Photos are never uploaded anywhere other than back to your own Google Drive. Face and object detection run on your device.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or user profiling, and your data is never sold or shared.
  • You can sign out and revoke access at any time, and clear all data the app keeps on your device.

1. Who we are and scope

Displaai is operated by Melisa Abuin, an individual developer, who is the only party responsible for the app. This policy applies to the web app at displaai.mkdir.no and to this privacy page. It does not apply to the third-party services the app connects to (see Sharing, transfer and disclosure), which have their own policies. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Google Drive is a trademark of Google LLC.

Because the app runs only in your browser and the developer does not receive your data, the developer does not act as a controller or processor of your Google Drive content. Your browser, on your device, is where the processing happens.

2. Google user data the app accesses

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, the app asks for the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. Through it, the app accesses the following data. It is fetched directly from Google to your browser:

Data What it includes Why it is needed
Account email address The email address of the signed-in Google account Shown in the sidebar so you can see which account is in use
Folders Names, identifiers and the hierarchy of your Drive folders Browsing, connecting folders and building views
Photo and video metadata Names, identifiers, types, sizes, dates, parent folders, dimensions, capture time, thumbnail links and embedded location (GPS) metadata Galleries, timeline, sorting, and showing photos on the map
Photo and video content The files you open or edit Displaying them in full size, playing videos, on-device detection and editing
Tags Custom properties that the app itself writes on your files to store the tags you add Tagging and filtering photos
Drive change history The list of files that changed since the last time the app synced Keeping the cached index of your library up to date

The app only requests the data it needs for these features, and only lists images, videos and folders. It does not read documents, spreadsheets or other file types for any purpose.

3. What the app does not collect

  • The developer does not receive any of your Google account data, photos, videos, tags, locations or settings.
  • The app does not ask for your name, password, phone number, address or payment details. Your Google password is entered only on Google’s own sign-in page and is never seen by the app.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and does not build profiles about you.
  • The app does not request your device’s location. Locations shown on the map come only from the metadata already inside your own photos.

4. How the app uses Google user data

The data is used only to provide the features you see in the app:

  • Displaying your photos and videos in galleries, folders and views.
  • Showing photos that have location metadata on a map.
  • Letting you add and remove tags, which are saved as private app properties on the Drive file.
  • Letting you edit a photo and save the result, either replacing the original file or saving a new file, and renaming or moving photos to the Drive trash when you ask for it. The app changes your files only as a direct result of an action you take.
  • Optional face and object detection, which suggests labels. This runs on your device; your photos are not sent to any server for it.

Full Drive access is needed because the editor must be able to replace the contents of an existing photo, rename it and trash it, which the narrower Drive permissions do not allow for files the app did not create. The app does not use this permission for anything outside the features listed above.

5. Consent and legal basis

The app accesses your Google Drive only with your explicit consent, given on Google’s own consent screen when you sign in. Without that consent the app cannot read any of your data. You can withdraw consent at any time by revoking access (see Retention and deletion). Withdrawing consent does not affect your Drive content, which stays in your account.

6. Data stored on your device

To work faster and remember your preferences, the app stores the following in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). This data stays on your device and is never sent to the developer:

Data Where Purpose
Google access token and its expiry time Local storage Keeps you signed in until the token expires (about an hour)
Cached index of your photo and folder metadata IndexedDB Opens your library quickly and syncs only the changes
Cached API responses (for example, your email address) Local storage Avoids repeating the same requests to Google
Settings, connected folders and views you created Local storage Remembers your preferences

Signing out clears the cached photo index and cached responses, removes the stored token and revokes it with Google. You can remove everything else, such as your settings, by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. Signing out and clearing this site’s data are the supported ways to remove it.

7. Security

  • All communication between the app and Google, the map tile provider and the model host happens over HTTPS.
  • The access token is short-lived, is kept only in your browser, and is sent only to Google’s APIs. The app never receives a client secret or a long-lived refresh token.
  • The app has no server and no developer-side database, so there is no central store of user data that could be breached.
  • Data kept in your browser is protected by your device and browser security. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile could see it, so sign out on shared or public computers.
  • The source code is public, so anyone can review how your data is handled.

8. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not allow humans to read it. The only services your browser contacts are:

Service Why What is sent
Google (Drive API, Google Identity Services and Google’s image servers) Sign-in, reading and editing your Drive files, and loading your thumbnails and photos Requests you make to your own Drive, authorized by your token. Google also sees your IP address and browser details, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy
Google Cloud Storage (MediaPipe models) Downloading the detection models used by on-device detection A standard download request, plus your IP address; no photos or account data
Stadia Maps / OpenStreetMap contributors Map tiles for the map view Standard tile requests (which map tiles are visible) and your IP address; no photos or account data
Static web hosting (GitHub Pages) Serving the app’s files to your browser Standard web requests, which may be logged by the host (for example your IP address and browser type); no photos or account data

These providers act independently and are governed by their own privacy policies. Your photos, tags, email address and Drive file names are never sent to any provider other than Google.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

Displaai’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not transferred to others, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with your consent.
  • It is not used for serving advertisements.
  • It is not read by humans, because the developer has no access to it.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models. The on-device detection models are pre-trained and are not trained on your photos.

10. Retention and deletion

The developer does not collect or retain your data, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Your photos and videos stay in your Google Drive and are never copied elsewhere by the app. Data the app keeps on your device (see Data stored on your device) is kept until you sign out, reset the app or clear this site’s data. The access token expires on its own after about an hour. You can:

  • Sign out in the app’s Settings to revoke access and clear the cache.
  • Remove the app’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • Remove the tags the app wrote by removing them in the app; they are stored only as private properties on your own Drive files and are removed together with a file if you delete it.
  • Photos you moved to the trash through the app can be restored from the Google Drive trash until Google empties it.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port it, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the developer does not hold your data, you exercise these directly:

  • Your Drive content and account data are managed in your Google Account and Google Drive, under Google’s own tools and policies.
  • Data kept by the app is on your device and you can view, change or delete it using your browser’s settings or the app’s Settings.
  • The app does not sell or share personal information and does not use it for targeted advertising or automated decision-making.

If you have a question or request about this policy, see Contact. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Cookies

The app does not set its own cookies and does not use cookies for tracking. It uses your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB as described above. Google sign-in and the map tile provider are loaded from their own domains and may set or read cookies according to their own policies.

13. International data transfers

The developer does not transfer your data, because it is never received. Your browser communicates directly with the services listed in Sharing, transfer and disclosure, whose servers may be located in different countries. How those providers handle data is described in their own policies.

14. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children. Signing in requires a Google account, which is subject to Google’s own age requirements.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because the app adds a feature that uses data differently, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Any new use of your Google data that goes beyond what is described here will require your consent again.

16. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/melisa-abuin/drive-photos-displayer. The source code is public, so you can verify how the app handles your data.
